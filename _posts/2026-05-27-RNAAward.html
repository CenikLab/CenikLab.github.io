We are delighted to share that Can has been selected for The RNA Society Moderna Award for Biomedical Innovation in RNA. The award recognizes innovative and enabling contributions to the use or function of RNA in biomedicine, disease processes, and treatments, and is presented to researchers in the first ten years as independent scientists.

This recognition belongs to the entire lab — past and present trainees, staff, and our wonderful collaborators — whose creativity, persistence, and care made this body of work possible.
